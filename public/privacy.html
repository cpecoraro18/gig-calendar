Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Calendar is a web app for viewing and editing your Google calendars on a phone, publishing events to a calendar your own website reads, and making images of your upcoming gigs to share. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no server behind it that receives, stores or sees your data.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you are asked to grant two permissions:

  • View and edit events on all your calendars (calendar.events) — to show your events, and to create, edit, move and delete them when you ask it to, including publishing an event to a calendar you choose as your gigs calendar.
  • See your calendars (calendar.readonly) — to list which calendars you have, their names and their colours.

The app does not ask for your contacts, email, files or anything outside Google Calendar. It cannot create, delete or share whole calendars.

How your data is used

Your calendar data travels directly between Google and your browser. It is used only to display your calendars and to make the changes you make in the app. It is never sent to the app's developer or to any third party, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train any model.

Photos you add to a social post stay on your device. They are drawn into the image in your browser and are never uploaded.

What is stored, and where

  • Your Google access token is kept in your browser's session storage and is discarded when you close the tab or sign out. It expires on its own after about an hour.
  • Your preferences — which calendars are shown, your default calendar, month or agenda view, your gigs calendar and your last social post style — are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • The app's code is cached on your device so it opens quickly. No calendar data is cached.

There are no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

Removing access

Sign out in the app to discard the token. To revoke the app's permission entirely, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clearing your browser's site data removes your saved preferences.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
